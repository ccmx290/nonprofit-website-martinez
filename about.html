Who We Are

Atelier Colima is a collective made up of artists born and raised in Colima, and we come from all over the state with a variety skills across all artistic fields. We share the goal of spreading the creative spirit to our local communities.

Through classes and events held at cultural centers and locally-owned cafes, galleries, and other locales, we share our knowledge and passion eagerly with those seeking to learn, have fun, and explore. Everyone is invited to Atelier Colima, and we welcome anyone who comes with open arms.

What We Do

Beyond teaching new skills to our friends and neighbors, Atelier Colima has raised funds, supplies, and attention for local artists, allowing them to work full-time in their creative fields and develop their professional artistic careers.

Moreover, we provide supportive and safe creative spaces for kids and teenagers. Based on the popularity of our 17-and-under events, there is a true need and demand for artistic endeavors that target our local youths, which Atelier Colima is more than happy to be able to provide.

Another key aspect of connecting our communities to the arts is ensuring that people have the resources they need to be creative. Limited funds and opportunities leave people without the ability to explore their interests and develop their skills. And while we can't provide the newest, most expensive, and highest quality materials, we fundraise and accept donations that allow us to 1) give away supplies (such as drawing tablets, markers, canvases, etc.) to those who can't afford them otherwise, and 2) offer free classes in mediums that are even more out of reach, such as music lessons.
