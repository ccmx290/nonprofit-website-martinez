About Donations

A great way to support our efforts is by donating, and we take both monetary donations and supply donations. It is through the generous contributions of our supporters that we can host our events/classes and provide materials to members of our communities, so truly, anything helps.

If you can't contribute in this way, volunteering is a great third option that you can learn more about in our Events page. And if that isn't a doable option either, rest assured that we are also endlessly grateful for those who support us by showing up to our events or spreading the word about the work that we do.

Monetary Donations

We are grateful for any money you decide to send our way, and below we describe the many ways that the money will be used, so you know that it will go to a good cause that you can support.

How we use your donations:

  • Fund our events/classes
  • Purchase new supplies
  • Repair or replace musical instruments and digital cameras as needed
  • Offer free/discounted meals during certain events/classes

Supply Donations

When talking about donating supplies, we accept those that are in good (but used), like new, and new condition. We understand wanting old, unused materials to go to a good cause, but our community deserves supplies that are clean and in working order.

We will not take items including, but not limited to: broken coloring supplies, dried-out markers, musical instruments that need repairs beyond tuning or restringing, or any damaged electronics (such as drawing tablets and digital cameras).

How we use donated supplies:

  • Donated to members of the community directly
  • Donated to local schools, places of worship, etc
  • Provided for use at our events/classes
